dm-crypt mappings:
  gsdf-rootfs:        linear, mmcblk0p57 @ offset 38192 sectors (NOT encrypted)

Rootfs details:
  Type: squashfs v4.0, xz compressed
  Size: ~81MB (8060 inodes)
  Mount: / (read-only)
  Overlay: /overlay via decrypted_systemrw (read-write)
  /etc overlay: overlay_etc (lowerdir=/etc, upperdir=/overlay/overlayfs/etc-upper)
